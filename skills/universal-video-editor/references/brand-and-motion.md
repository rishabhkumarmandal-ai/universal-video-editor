Headshot panels: opaque black, blue or grey border, solid light-grey icon tile, dark-blue glyph. SVG transparency is fine **inside a solid tile**; the tile and settled panel themselves must not have alpha below 1. Do not recolour the camera footage to match the brand. Monochrome brand marks may follow the palette when appropriate; keep source provenance.

At 1080×1920, captions are approximately 72–80px Poppins Bold, 2–3 words, lower torso, with a dark outline on camera footage. Blue emphasis on the currently spoken keyword; dark blue on light scenes. Keep negations meaningful: “par slot nahi” and “koi prompt nahi” belong together. Never transplant those words into a different script. Avoid captions becoming tooltips about implementation. Preserve every intended spoken word unless speech editing is explicitly requested.

Headshot labels were above the head in the accepted video because the face occupied much of the middle. For other footage inspect the actual head, hands, objects and existing text. Use larger blank regions or a full-screen explanation instead of covering the subject. Treat bottom/right platform-safe margins as adjustable defaults for the target platform, not a guarantee from a fixed rectangle.

## Two complementary reference families

**Clean UI (ref-1):** off-white/grey cards, rounded icon tiles, restrained shadows, one dominant heading, a small diagram or UI example, short builds and a return to the speaker. Borrow the hierarchy and spacing. Avoid a wall of identical cards for unrelated messages.

**Conceptual motion (ref-3):** staged typography, layered cutout objects, bar/playhead movement, cause/effect diagrams and fan/segment reveals. Use real supplied/licensed imagery where it clarifies an idea. With no image assets, vector icons/shapes are valid. Palette stays blue/black/grey. A 300 counter, banknotes or portraits visible in the reference are unrelated source content, not permission to reuse the claim or person.

Use icon paths/orbits, sliders, timelines, stepped list builds, compare/swap and typing according to the new words. Counts must come from the script/data or a clearly labelled structural list. No invented percentages or provider features.

Typical motion: 0.3–0.4s entrance, short movement, brief scale settle, path draw or a stagger. Vary form/easing deliberately. Leave read time after the build. Stillness can be intentional; do not add constant bouncing to every caption. Full-screen explanations normally run about 2.5–5s, but the script governs. Never force a fixed cutaway quota onto a short or unrelated video.

The accepted speaker/cutaway boundaries are direct cuts. In HyperFrames author each cutaway independently when following that rhythm, or use explicit transitions within a multi-scene graphic composition. Do not create empty frames by fading all content out before a transition.

Original voice; restrained pops/clicks/whooshes tied to selected graphic actions. Music optional, instrumental by default for this creator, licensed and below speech. No music is better than an unsourced or distracting bed. Measure final loudness/peak; initial reference target around -16 LUFS and <= -1dBTP, adjusted for destination.
